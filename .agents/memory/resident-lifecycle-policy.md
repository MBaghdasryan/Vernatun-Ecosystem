---
name: Resident lifecycle policy
description: Restoration-compatible renter access and common-area maintenance acceptance decisions.
---

For restored renters with no recorded lease for their unit, preserve legacy access. Once any lease is recorded, only a currently effective, noncancelled period grants protected access; use the Asia/Yerevan calendar.

**Why:** Turning on lease enforcement must not silently lock out restored renter accounts whose archive never supplied lease periods. Recording a contract explicitly activates the date-based lifecycle.

**How to apply:** Preserve this activation boundary when changing rent/occupancy workflows. Do not grant former renters historical documents/receipts access without an explicit policy.

Common-area equipment maintenance uses a building-scoped system request and closes through independent manager verification, not resident confirmation.

**Why:** Common equipment has no genuine apartment or resident author; inventing either misattributes records and can expose work through apartment permissions.

**How to apply:** Keep the shared WorkOrder flow, real system provenance, and scoped access when adding maintenance sources or technician features.

New resident directory, discussions, private chats, property listings and service catalogs are explicit building-level opt-ins, separate from ordinary restored account access.

**Why:** Legacy access preservation must not silently publish residents' identities or enable new community/service surfaces for existing buildings. Enabling a section also cannot override module, role, lease or privacy restrictions.

**How to apply:** Keep an authorized staff setup step and privacy-aware filtering when expanding resident features; never treat an enabled building section as authorization by itself.

Discussion support is informal, not a legal vote. Converting a topic requires an explicitly confirming operational employee; organization administration alone is not operational execution authority.

**Why:** The product requires real requests or proposals within existing governance, without a second Workflow/voting engine or a fabricated apartment/resident author for shared-area work.

**How to apply:** Keep publication and owner eligibility in existing governance. Draft meeting/agenda metadata must remain private even through discussion backlinks and action/list payloads; a backlink is not permission to inspect an unpublished meeting. Cancellation is not evidence of publication: a meeting can be cancelled before it was ever published. Preserve building-scoped system provenance for shared-area requests and do not grant operational staff resident-settings or private-chat administration just to process a discussion.
