---
name: Scoped staff availability
description: Availability is a scoped planning assessment, not proof of company-wide free time.
---

Treat staff availability as a claim about the selected interval and the caller's
authorized scope. Missing or incomplete shifts mean unknown, never free.
Planning records apply to a building or a unit; a unit-scoped manager must not
change a building-wide plan.

**Why:** A restricted dispatcher may not see an employee's visits at other
properties. Calling that employee globally available would turn limited access
into a misleading scheduling decision. Building/unit absence records also do not
constitute company-wide leave approval.

**How to apply:** Keep the scope qualification visible. Check employee assignment
validity for the entire planned interval, not just today. Do not turn a positive
status into automatic booking or a global conflict guarantee. Company-wide leave,
cross-scope booking enforcement and HR attendance require their own explicit model.
